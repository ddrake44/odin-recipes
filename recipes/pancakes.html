Hash Browns PB & J

Nobody will pan these pancakes.

Why buy frozen pancakes when you could have fresh and homemade pancakes in a matter of minutes.

With only two ingredients, one from the cubord, one from the fridge and one bowl. Mix the ingredients together and watch puffy panakes come to life on a hot pan.

Follow these simple steps and you can begin munching.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup of Bobs Red Mill pancake mix
  • 1/4 cup of water
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Grab a mixing bowl.
  2. Dump Bobs Red Mill Pancake Mix into mixing bowl.
  3. Dump water into mixing bowl
  4. Mix the two ingredients until wet, smooth but has body.
