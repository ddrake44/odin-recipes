This is the most challenging recipes on our website. Appears to be easy, or simple, right? No. Proper proportions are crucical.

And we are the only ones that can share those magic proportions for the ultimate PB & J.

Ingredients:

  • Your favorite sliced bread
  • Your favorite crunchy PeanutButter
  • Your favorite jam

Instructions

  1. Choose two of the best looking slices. Make sure to feel each one to make sure you are choosing the best.
  2. Put 1/4 cup of your favoirte peanut butter on one slice of bread.
  3. Put a healthy 1//8 cup of your favorite jelly on the other slice od bread.
  4. Take the two covered slices and put one ontop of the other. Important to note, you will want to place the sides of the bread that have peanut butter or jelly together. If these sides end up on the outside of the sandwich, you will have a mess. Trust me.
