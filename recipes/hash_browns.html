How to make the best hash browns

In no time at all, have a delicious bowl of hash browns, prepped, cooked, and consumed by friends and family.

Simple ingredient, multiple colors, harmonious flavors, and a little bit of something spicy keep the fork, the hand, and the mouth busy till the plate is empty.

The ingredients needed:

  • 1 Japanese sweet potatoes
  • 1 Yellow Onion
  • 1 Poblano Pepper
  • 1/2 bunch of cale.
  • salt to taste
  • pepper to taste.

Follow these simple steps to find flavor town:

  1. Heat pan on low-medium while ingredients are prepped for 5+ minutes.
  2. Cut and dice onions into bite sized pieces.
  3. Cut and dice potatoes into bite sized pieces.
  4. Put diced potatoes in the microwave for 3 minutes (or until they are soft enough to be squishy to the touch).
  5. Heat oil in a hot pan (30 seconds). Genlty place diced onions into the pan.
  6. Take par cooked poatatoes out of microwave and place them on the pan with the onions.
  7. Take diced kale and place in pan. Cook for 2 minutes.
  8. Take diced poblano and placed in pan. Cook for another 2 minutes.
  9. After all the ingredients are in the pan. Cook for another 10 minutes or longer. Longer the dish cooks, the more flavor their will be.
